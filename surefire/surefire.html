Training

AI Manifestation, in one day

Your good scenario arrives in phases, on a clock. A funder says yes, the pilot works, then demand triples overnight. Your team makes the calls that success forces on it: who owns it, what scales, who gets the credit, and who gets left out. You leave with a possibility register, your three scenarios with owners and dates, and a first experiment already run.

SustainingKeeping the benefit going after the launch

Each capacity is scored dreamed → tried → repeated → proven. To claim "proven", you name the date and the evidence.
